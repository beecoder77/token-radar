How a value is built

Each protocol’s implied value comes from the snapshot itself, never from a hand-picked list of names. impliedValue() fits on the traded protocols in launchable categories, then multiplies the curve by a shrunk category offset, by 7-day TVL growth, by seasoning (how long it has run with no token) and by chain breadth. The bands are prediction intervals — curve uncertainty plus the scatter between protocols — so they are wide: the ranking, not the point value, is the signal. farmPlan() writes each protocol’s concrete steps with their dollar cost and minutes, and verdictFor() nets the plan against the launch odds: a launch is assumed to hand 5% of the implied value to early users, split among an estimated farmer count (TVL / $25,000, clamped 500–200,000), with a farmer’s slice scaling with the square root of the deposit.

implied = 10^(a + b·log10 TVL) × 10^offset × growth × age × chains

growth = 1 + clamp(7d change, −15%, +25%) ÷ 100  ·  age = 1 + min(years listed, 3) × 0.03  ·  chains = 1 + min(chains − 1, 4) × 0.02  ·  offset = shrunk category residual, ±0.3 dex max  ·  launch odds = category base + watchlist 5pts + up to 6pts age + 3pts at $500M+ TVL + 3pts on 7d growth over 10%, clamped 5–60%

verdict: FARM when launch odds clear 1.5× the break-even odds (plan cost ÷ slice-if-launch) and the net per active hour clears $20; MARGINAL when launch odds clear break-even; else SKIP. Most rows are SKIP — a radar that says no is doing its job.

Data: the sealed 2026-11-14 DefiLlama snapshot (758 protocols at or above $5M TVL). Implied values are this radar’s own estimates from that snapshot — not predictions that a token will launch, or what it would be worth if it did. A protocol is shown as recording no token, never as having none: its DefiLlama record may simply not carry one. Nothing here is financial advice.
